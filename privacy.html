Privacy policy.

Plain words about what we collect, why, and what we never do with it.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

RESHAM ("we", "our", operated by Digigrow Solution) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information this storefront collects when you browse, save items to a wishlist, place an order or write to us — and the care we take with it.

1. Information we collect

  • Order information. When you place an order we collect your name, email address, phone number and shipping address. We need these to deliver your parcel and to send you updates about it.
  • Bag and wishlist. The items in your bag and the pieces you heart are stored in your own browser (local storage on your device). They are not linked to any account and are never sent to us until you place an order.
  • Payment information. This storefront never sees or stores card numbers, UPI PINs or bank credentials. Payments run on the payment gateway's own secure page, governed by the gateway's privacy policy.
  • Messages. If you write to us through the contact form or WhatsApp, we receive what you choose to send.

2. How we use information

  • To pack, ship and deliver your order, and to reverse-pick-up a return.
  • To send order confirmations, delivery updates and, if something goes wrong, a phone call.
  • To answer your questions and handle returns or refunds.
  • Occasionally, with your consent, to send a note about a new collection. Every such message carries a one-tap unsubscribe.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. Not ever.

3. What we share, and with whom

  • Logistics partners receive your name, address and phone number — only what the courier needs to deliver.
  • Payment gateway processes your payment directly; we receive only a success or failure signal and a reference number.
  • Law. If a court or lawful authority requires it, we comply with valid legal process.

4. Cookies and local storage

This site stores a small amount of data in your browser so that your bag survives a page refresh: your cart contents, your wishlist, and your last applied coupon. You can clear this at any time from your browser settings; clearing it empties your bag and wishlist but affects nothing else. We do not run advertising trackers.

5. Data retention and security

Order records are kept only as long as needed for accounting, warranty and legal requirements, then deleted. Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS), and access to order information inside our studio is limited to the people who pack and dispatch.

6. Your choices

  • Ask us for a copy of the information we hold about your orders.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your order information, where law permits.
  • Unsubscribe from marketing messages at any time.

7. Demonstration notice

This storefront is a demonstration build by Digigrow Solution. No real payment is processed and no real dispatch takes place; any information entered during a demo order stays in your own browser and is not transmitted to a server.
