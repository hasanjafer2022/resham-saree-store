Shipping & delivery.

From our loom-side packing table in Lucknow to your door.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Every parcel leaves Lucknow folded in muslin, wrapped in tissue and boxed — the way a saree should travel.

1. Dispatch time

Ready-stock pieces are packed and handed to the courier within 24–48 working hours of your order confirmation. Bridal sets that involve matching blouse stitching may take 5–7 working days; the product page says so clearly where it applies.

2. Delivery estimates

DestinationTypical transit after dispatch
Lucknow & UP cities1–2 days
Delhi NCR, metros (Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune)2–3 days
Most other pincodes3–5 days
North-East, J&K, island and remote pin codes5–8 days

You can check the estimate for your pincode on any product page. During Diwali, wedding season peaks or monsoon disruptions, couriers everywhere slow down — we will tell you honestly if yours does.

3. Shipping fee

  • Free shipping on orders of ₹9,999 and above.
  • ₹199 flat below that, anywhere in India.
  • Cash on delivery is available across serviceable pin codes at no extra charge.

4. Tracking

The moment your parcel is scanned by the courier, the tracking link goes to you by email and WhatsApp. If a scan goes quiet for more than 48 hours, write to us — we chase couriers so you don't have to.

5. Changing the delivery address

Address changes are free until the parcel is dispatched. After dispatch, a redirect costs what the courier charges us (usually ₹0–150) and adds a day — message us as early as you can.

6. International delivery

Not yet. We ship within India only at present; international shipping is on the loom plan for next season.

7. Demonstration notice

This storefront is a demonstration build by Digigrow Solution; no physical parcel is dispatched. The timelines and fees above describe the production behaviour of the store.
