Returns & refunds.

Seven days, free pickup, money back — stated simply so you can trust it.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

If a drape does not feel right, we take it back. This page explains when, how, and exactly when your money returns.

1. The 7-day window

You may request a return within 7 days of delivery. The saree must be unworn and unwashed, with original tags attached, the fall and picot stitching untouched, and the blouse piece uncut. Jewellery must be unused with packaging intact.

2. What is not returnable

  • Pieces altered, stitched with fall-picot, or cut to length after delivery.
  • Custom or made-to-measure blouse orders noted as such on the product page.
  • Ear studs and items worn on the skin, for hygiene reasons — unless damaged or wrong on arrival.
  • Bulk and wholesale lots arranged over WhatsApp or email.

3. How to start a return

  1. Write to care@digigrowsolutions.in or WhatsApp us with your order number and one photo of the piece.
  2. We confirm eligibility within one working day and book a free reverse pickup at your address.
  3. Keep the piece folded as received, in its muslin and box, until the courier arrives.

4. Refund timelines

The refund begins once the piece reaches Lucknow and passes a one-person quality check — usually the day it arrives.

5. Exchange instead of refund

Prefer a different colour or size? Say so when you write to us — an exchange ships as soon as the original piece is picked up, subject to stock at the loom. Price differences are collected or refunded at parity.

6. Damaged, wrong or missing pieces

If a parcel arrives damaged, or the piece inside is not what you ordered, write to us within 48 hours of delivery with photographs. We replace it at our cost — outward, return and inward shipping all on us — or refund in full, your choice.

7. Cancellations

You may cancel an unshipped order for a full refund by writing to us before the dispatch email goes out. Once dispatched, the normal 7-day return flow applies.

8. Demonstration notice

This storefront is a demonstration build by Digigrow Solution; returns are simulated and no money is charged or refunded. The policy above describes the production behaviour of the store.

9. Common questions

How many days do I get to return a saree?
You have 7 days from the date of delivery. Write to care@digigrowsolutions.in with your order number and we arrange a free reverse pickup.

When will my money come back?
Refunds are issued within 48 hours of the piece passing inspection at our Lucknow studio. Banks post UPI refunds the same day; card refunds take 5–7 working days to reflect.

Is return shipping charged?
Never. Reverse pickup is free on every order across India.

Can I exchange instead of taking a refund?
Yes — an exchange ships as soon as the original piece is picked up, subject to stock, and any price difference is settled at parity.
